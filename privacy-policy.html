Privacy Policy

How 4M AUTOTECH and Khawaja Garib Nawaz Car Detailing handle, protect, and respect your personal information.

Last Updated: August 2026

4M AUTOTECH ("we", "us", or "our"), an automotive brand of Khawaja Garib Nawaz Car Detailing, is committed to safeguarding the privacy of our website visitors, customers, and partners.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with our website or contact us via WhatsApp, phone, or email:

  • Contact Details: Full name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, and doorstep location/address.
  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle make, model, year, and requested detailing services or chemical products.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous browsing behavior, device information, browser type, and interaction metrics to improve website performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To provide, process, and confirm product orders and doorstep service bookings.
  • To respond promptly to customer enquiries, quotation requests, and technical consultations.
  • To maintain quality control, improve service satisfaction, and send service reminders upon request.
  • To ensure legal compliance and protect against fraudulent activities.

3. WhatsApp & Direct Communication

When you click WhatsApp or Call buttons on our website, you connect directly to our customer support team (+91 99997 46003). We do not send unsolicited spam messages, and your phone number is never shared with third-party telemarketers.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement stringent industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only when necessary to comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful governmental requests, or protect our legal rights.
